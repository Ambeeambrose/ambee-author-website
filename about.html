ABOUT ME

Hello, I'm Ambee Ambrose, the pen name under which I publish my work as a novelist and independent author.

My interest in storytelling began when I was young. I have always enjoyed turning the things people experience-love, difficult choices, family struggles, betrayal, hope, and the unexpected turns of life-into stories.

One thing I enjoy most about writing is creating scenes readers can picture clearly. I want them to see the places, understand the characters, and feel close to what is happening.

Long before I began publishing, friends enjoyed listening to my stories. Whenever I told a story in my own unique way, they would often smile and say, "That's an Ambee Ambrose story." Over time, the name stayed with me, and it eventually became the pen name under which I publish my books today.

I don't limit myself to one kind of story. I write emotional novels, crime thrillers, family conflicts, and stories inspired by real life. Whatever the subject, I want readers to close the final page with something meaningful to reflect on.

Away from writing, I enjoy spending time with my family and listening to music, especially jazz. Inspiration often comes from ordinary moments that many people overlook.

Thank you for visiting my website. I hope you discover a story you'll enjoy and remember long after you've finished reading.
