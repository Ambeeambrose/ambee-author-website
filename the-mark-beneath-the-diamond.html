The Mark Beneath the Diamond

A Crime Thriller Where Every Clue Matters

Book Description

The truth was hidden beneath the diamond.

On the night Elena Hart is murdered during a violent robbery, the only thing taken from her body is a priceless gold necklace crowned with a flawless diamond. Believing they have erased every trace, the killers disappear into the shadows.

Months later, grieving widower Daniel Hart is stunned to see the same necklace worn by a woman attending an exclusive business gala. He knows instantly it belongs to his late wife. Rather than take matters into his own hands, Daniel turns to the law, setting in motion an investigation that uncovers a dangerous criminal network hidden behind a seemingly legitimate jewelry business.

As detectives unravel the mystery, a tiny engraving hidden beneath the diamond becomes the key to exposing a web of greed, deception, and murder. Piece by piece, the truth emerges until every lie begins to collapse.

The Mark Beneath the Diamond is a gripping crime thriller filled with mystery, suspense, and relentless justice, proving that even the smallest clue can expose the darkest secrets.
