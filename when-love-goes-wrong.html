When Love Goes Wrong

A Heartbreaking Tale of Love and Consequences

Book Description

Sandra has everything she ever dreamed of, a successful career, a devoted loving husband, and two beautiful children. But an unexpected encounter with someone from her past draws her into a web of temptation, deception, and betrayal.

One careless decision changes everything, leaving broken hearts, shattered trust, and a family forced to face the painful cost of betrayal. As she confronts the consequences of her choices, she begins a journey of regret, reflection, and the hope of finding forgiveness.

Meanwhile, George struggles to rebuild his life while the past continues to cast a long shadow over everyone he loves.

Can forgiveness heal what has been broken?
Can a shattered life ever truly be restored?

When Love Goes Wrong is an emotional drama about love, betrayal, forgiveness, and the consequences of the choices we make. Perfect for readers who enjoy realistic relationships, family conflict, and stories that leave a lasting impression.
