Child Abuse: The Story of Busy Parents

A Powerful Family Drama with a Life-Changing Message

Book Description

A powerful short story about trust, family, and the lasting consequences of the busiest choices parents make.

Child Abuse: The Story of Busy Parents is a powerful and emotionally moving short story about trust, family, and the hidden consequences of neglect. As life's pressures consume a family, one painful decision changes a child's future forever, revealing how the busiest choices can leave the deepest scars.

Though brief, this story delivers a thought-provoking message about parental responsibility, love, and the importance of being present in the lives of our children. It is a reminder that some mistakes cannot be undone, but awareness can help prevent them.
