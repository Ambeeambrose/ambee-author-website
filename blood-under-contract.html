Blood Under Contract

A Gripping Crime Thriller

Book Description

Murder is no longer an act of rage. It is a signed agreement.

When a series of calculated killings begins to surface across Lagos, Detective Ibrahim Kiki discovers that the victims were not chosen at random. Behind every murder lies a hidden contract network where human lives are bought, sold, and terminated for a price.

As Kiki and his elite investigative team follow a trail of coded payments, secret agreements, and powerful connections, they uncover a sophisticated criminal organization operating across borders and protected by influential figures. With every step closer to the truth, they become targets of a syndicate that silences anyone who stands in its way.

As the body count rises, Kiki must risk everything, his career, his team, and even his own life to expose a system where loyalty is bought, betrayal is inevitable, and blood is the final signature.

Blood Under Contract is the explosive first novel in the Detective Ibrahim Kiki thriller series—a gripping story of corruption, organized crime, and one detective's relentless pursuit of justice.
