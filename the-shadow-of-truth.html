The Shadow of Truth

A Suspense Thriller of Lies and Hidden Truths

Book Description

One man. One secret. A nation built on lies.

When Samuel Kalu witnesses a murder meant to disappear into the shadows, he becomes the next target of a system that kills quietly and rewrites history just as easily. What begins as a desperate fight for survival quickly turns into a dangerous battle against powerful forces determined to silence him forever.

Pursued by corrupt officials, hidden power networks, and men willing to sacrifice innocent lives to protect their secrets, Samuel soon realizes that nowhere is safe. As betrayal comes from those he trusts most, every decision draws him closer to the truth, and closer to death.

In a world where lies travel faster than justice, Samuel must decide whether to disappear and save himself or risk everything to expose a conspiracy capable of shaking an entire nation.

The Shadow of Truth is a gripping political crime thriller packed with suspense, conspiracy, corruption, and relentless tension. A powerful story where loyalty is fragile, power is deadly, and justice comes at a brutal cost.
