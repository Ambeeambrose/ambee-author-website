The Price of a Dream

A Powerful Story of Dreams, Choices, and Consequences

Book Description

At twenty-one, Paulina believed freedom was something money could buy. A deeply emotional story about dreams, temptation, and the life-changing consequences of one decision.

Raised in a modest but loving home, she dreams of the glamorous life she sees around her, fast cars... endless parties, and the effortless confidence of those who seem to have everything. When an unexpected friendship opens the door to quick money and instant success, Paulina convinces herself she has found a harmless shortcut to the life she has always wanted.

What begins as a simple decision soon becomes a turning point that changes the course of her life. As ambition grows into habit and silence replaces caution, the future she once imagined slowly begins to unravel.

Years later, love, marriage, and the hope of motherhood force Paulina to confront a painful truth she can no longer escape. She discovers that every dream has a price, and some choices leave consequences that last a lifetime.

The Price of a Dream is a deeply emotional novel about youth, temptation, hope, and the lasting consequences of uninformed choices. Told in an intimate first-person voice, it is not a story of judgment, it is a story of awareness, redemption, and the courage to face the truth.
